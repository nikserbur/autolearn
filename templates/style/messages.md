# Message style - learned from approved drafts and the user's own edits

Read by the `colleague-message` skill. Rules first, then evidence. Keep at most 10 examples.
Empty until you approve or edit drafts. A starter set is in the comments; uncomment what fits.

## Rules
<!-- starter rules:
- A greeting, then straight to the point: one or two short sentences.
- A link or ticket key when the recipient needs it to act.
- Do not explain our plans, readiness or deadlines unless the recipient needs them to act. The
  message itself is the ping; end with a neutral question answerable in one line.
- Access requests: context in one phrase, what I have and what I lack, the exact username or
  resource, a question with a ready option.
-->
